Atlas Notes
Vascular atlas. Endothelial cells (Cap_EC, Art_EC, Ven_EC), pericytes, SMCs, fibroblasts. No astrocytes, microglia, or neurons — those parenchymal cell types were not enriched in the source datasets.
Gene space. Expression data covers 16,755 human genes with at least one species ortholog (v4 atlas). Species missing an ortholog show no data (null) for that gene. A handful of genes with no usable ortholog map (e.g. ABCB1) still surface curated info via the Pathways panel and external links.
Conservation. Rat/pig scores are unreliable (only 2 shared cell types with human). Macaque excludes Cap_EC.

Expressolog Conservation vs Human
How this is computed. For each species, we take the vector of mean expression × cell type alongside human's vector restricted to shared cell types only, and compute Pearson r. r ≈ 1 means expression pattern across cell types matches human; r ≈ −1 means the pattern is inverted; r ≈ 0 means uncorrelated.
Rat & pig: Conservation scores unreliable — only 2 shared cell types with human (Cap_EC, Pericyte) → Pearson r is mathematically forced to ±1.

Cell-type specificity (τ index). τ = Σᵢ(1 − x̂ᵢ) / (n − 1) where x̂ᵢ is mean expression normalized to its max across cell types. τ ≈ 1 means highly specific to one cell type; τ ≈ 0 means broadly expressed (Yanai et al. Bioinformatics 2005). Computed per species; shown for selected species.
